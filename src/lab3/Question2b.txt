OpenCV (segmentation) is much more stiff because there is no training. 
It's mathematically deterministic. It doesn't require any training/validation datasets, and 
works just off of any image it's given. This makes it much lighter than YOLO.

YOLO can generalize off of many images given as its training data, and thus is less stiff. It can
still detect things that don't exactly fit the parameters and also generates confidence scores for it's detections.
It can be made more accurate with larger datasets
